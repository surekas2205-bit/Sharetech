# ResourceShare — 60-second demo pitch

Hello judges. Our project is ResourceShare, a trusted resource-sharing workflow for colleges and community organisations.

Valuable resources like laptops, cameras, labs and meeting rooms may be unused, while students and project teams cannot afford to buy or rent them. A simple booking calendar is not enough: institutions also need eligibility checks, owner approval, fair access and clear condition records.

ResourceShare lets an owner list a resource with its location, availability and access requirements. A student can discover it and request access with a purpose and time. The owner reviews the request, then the handoff can be documented with check-in and return condition notes. Delays or damage can be reported, and actions are recorded in an activity history.

This prototype uses fictional data and demonstrates the core journey. For production, we would add secure authentication, a shared database, calendar conflict detection, notifications and institution-approved verification. Safety and penalty decisions would remain subject to human review.

Thank you.
