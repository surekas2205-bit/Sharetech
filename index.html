About ResourceShare

Built for safe, fair sharing

ResourceShare helps colleges and communities share equipment and spaces through requests, owner approval, condition records and a transparent activity log.

This is a hackathon demo prototype. It uses fictional records. Login and roles are not real security controls.

Fair access principles

  • Eligibility requirements are visible before a request.
  • Owners review requests rather than allowing automatic access.
  • Waiting-list decisions should follow published rules.
  • Safety, damage and penalty decisions require human review.
